## 📊 Tokenomics & Security
VoltPay implements a secure, non-inflationary economic model with a fixed total supply of **200,000,000 VLT**.

**Distribution:**
* **50%** - Strategic Reserve (Locked)
* **35%** - Ecosystem & Presale Liquidity
* **10%** - Founder / Personal Hold
* **5%** - Team (Vesting: 3-month cliff, 12-month linear)

**Fee Structure (50/50 Split):**
* **2% Buy Fee / 4% Sell Fee**
* 50% automatically converts to PancakeSwap Liquidity (Auto-LP).
* 50% directed to Marketing & Development (VoltAI, user acquisition).

*Anti-whale and early-stage protection mechanisms (Max TX / Max Wallet) are hardcoded into the contract to ensure a stable launch environment.*

## 📱 Upcoming Ecosystem Expansion
The VLT token serves as the foundation for the upcoming VoltPay mobile wallet app, which will be released on **Apple App Store** and **Google Play**. The wallet will feature zero-fee internal transfers (gasless P2P transactions) and seamless VoltAI integration.
